# T01: Discounted prices round to the nearest cent

**Area:** `shop/pricing.py`, `apply_discount`

`apply_discount` truncates: `apply_discount(995, 10)` returns 895, but 90% of 995 cents is 895.5 cents, which our
price policy rounds to 896.

## Acceptance criteria

- The discounted price is rounded to the nearest cent, halves up: `apply_discount(995, 10) == 896`,
  `apply_discount(1001, 50) == 501`, `apply_discount(999, 15) == 849`.
- Percentages outside 0-100 still raise `ValueError`.
- No other function changes behaviour.
