# T02: Tax regions are case-insensitive, unknown ones give a clear error

**Area:** `shop/pricing.py`, `tax_for`

Region codes come from a form, so they arrive as `"us-ny"` or `" DE "`, and `tax_for` fails with a bare
`KeyError` for them and for regions we do not know.

## Acceptance criteria

- Region codes match `TAX_RATES` case-insensitively, ignoring surrounding whitespace:
  `tax_for(10000, " us-ny ") == 888`, `tax_for(1000, "de") == 190`.
- An unknown region raises `ValueError("unknown tax region: <region as given>")`, e.g.
  `ValueError("unknown tax region: XX")` for `"XX"`; never `KeyError`.
- Rounding and rates are unchanged.
