# T11: Shortened labels end with an ellipsis

**Area:** `shop/text.py`, `truncate`

Report labels are cut off mid-word with no sign that they were shortened.

## Acceptance criteria

- A text longer than `width` is cut and ends with `"..."`, and the result including the dots is at most `width`
  characters: `truncate("Stainless steel bottle", 10) == "Stainle..."`.
- Whitespace just before the dots is dropped: `truncate("Blue mug extra", 8) == "Blue..."`.
- A text that fits is returned unchanged. `width` is always at least 3.
