# T03: A second volume tier from 100 units

**Area:** `shop/pricing.py`, `bulk_price`

Wholesale customers get 15% off from 100 units of one product.

## Acceptance criteria

- 100 units or more: 15% off the total, rounded down to the cent like the existing tier:
  `bulk_price(100, 100) == 8500`, `bulk_price(99, 150) == 12622`.
- 10 to 99 units keep 10% off (`bulk_price(100, 99) == 8910`); fewer than 10 pay full price.
- A quantity of zero or less still raises `ValueError`.
