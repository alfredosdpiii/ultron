# T10: Clean product slugs

**Area:** `shop/text.py`, `slugify`

`slugify("  Blue  Mug (XL)! ")` gives `"--blue--mug--xl---"`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Runs of characters other than letters and digits become a single dash, and there is no leading or trailing
  dash: `slugify("  Blue  Mug (XL)! ") == "blue-mug-xl"`, `slugify("Tea & Coffee") == "tea-coffee"`.
- Letters are lower-cased; a name with no letters or digits gives `""`.
