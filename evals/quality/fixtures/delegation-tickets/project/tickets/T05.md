# T05: Reservations must be for a positive quantity

**Area:** `shop/inventory.py`, `reserve`

`reserve(stock, "MUG", -3)` currently adds three mugs to the stock.

## Acceptance criteria

- `reserve` with a quantity of zero or less raises `ValueError("quantity must be positive")` and leaves the stock
  unchanged (no key added either).
- Positive quantities behave as before, including `OutOfStock`.
