# T03: Split amounts fairly

**Area:** `ledger/money.py`, `split_evenly`

Today the last share takes the whole remainder.

## Acceptance criteria

- The remainder is spread one cent at a time over the first shares: `split_evenly(1000, 3) == [334, 333, 333]`,
  `split_evenly(1002, 4) == [251, 251, 250, 250]`.
- Shares always add up to the amount, and no two shares differ by more than one cent.
- `parts` of zero or less raises `ValueError`.
