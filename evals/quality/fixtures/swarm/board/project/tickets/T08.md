# T08: Monthly totals per month

**Area:** `ledger/statements.py`, `monthly_totals`

`monthly_totals` groups by year today, although it is used for monthly statements.

## Acceptance criteria

- Keys are `"YYYY-MM"`: `monthly_totals([{"date": "2026-01-31", "amount": 5}, {"date": "2026-02-01", "amount": 7}])
  == {"2026-01": 5, "2026-02": 7}`.
- Amounts in the same month are summed; months without transactions are absent.
