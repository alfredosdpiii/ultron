# T10: Wrap memos at word boundaries

**Area:** `ledger/text.py`, `wrap_memo`

`wrap_memo` cuts words in half today.

## Acceptance criteria

- Lines break between words; runs of spaces count as one: `wrap_memo("rent for  the month", 9) == ["rent for", "the month"]`.
- No line is longer than `width`; no line starts or ends with a space.
- A word longer than `width` is cut into pieces of `width` characters: `wrap_memo("abcdefghij kl", 4) == ["abcd", "efgh", "ij", "kl"]`.
- An empty or all-space memo gives `[""]`.
